# Knowledge Card Edition

The student-facing release has been replaced by a course-wide flashcard study mode.

- Modules covered: S1-S10.
- Knowledge cards: 981.
- S1-S3 source-reviewed expansion: S1 138, S2 518, S3 130 cards (about 3x their prior counts).
- Old 3,000-question exports, per-module quiz files, final exam, question-bank viewer, and seed-question exports have been removed from this release.
- Student entry: `html/index.html`.
- Offline data file: `html/cards-data.js`.
- Progress: browser-local, with JSON export/import.
- Review notes: `knowledge_cards_review.md`.

Course lessons and video scripts are retained as supplementary study materials, not as a replacement for the source-mapped cards or current standards. S3 lesson guidance on universal loads, spacing, safety factors, and unauthorized proof-loading was corrected in this release. See `knowledge_cards_review.md` for the S1-S3 source map and review limits. Safety, compliance, cable-sizing, and product-specific values must be checked against current local requirements before practical use.
